## 2 · Executive summary (open with this)

**Situation.** Agentforce and Financial Services Cloud are our biggest growth bets, but the story is only as good as the field's ability to *show* it. Today, a compelling demo requires an SE, a provisioned org, and a scheduled slot.

**Complication.** That gating throttles pipeline. SEs are our scarcest resource, demos land days after the moment of interest, and every rep tells the story a little differently — so message and quality vary deal to deal.

**Resolution.** This guided demo removes the gate. It's a single, zero-infrastructure web experience with **seven industry storylines** that walk the full Agentforce Service journey — from an AI co-worker planning the rep's day, to a live Agentforce Voice call, to a grounded resolution and a closed case. Any rep can brand it to the prospect and run it **on the spot**. It doubles as a **self-paced enablement tool**, and it **captures usage analytics** so we can see adoption, coverage, and which stories resonate.

**So what.** More demos, earlier in the cycle, told consistently and on-message — while freeing SE capacity for the high-value work only SEs can do.

---

## 3 · What it is (one slide)

- **7 storylines** across three industries — **Banking** (Retail Fraud, Wealth Onboarding, Commercial sFTP/ACH), **FinTech** (API Incident Escalation), **Insurance** (Multi-Carrier Quoting, Auto Claim/FNOL, Life Beneficiary Change).
- Each runs the **same three-act journey** so reps learn one flow and reuse it everywhere:
  - **Act 0 — Agentforce co-worker.** The rep's AI "Ask" companion plans the day, clusters open cases to a customer, and drafts a proactive outreach.
  - **Act 1 — Agentforce Voice.** A live, monitored voice call that verifies identity, captures intent, and warm-transfers to a human with full context.
  - **Act 2 — The rep + Service Assistant.** A grounded Service Plan, a Knowledge citation, and an on-brand email — resolved on first contact.
  - **Act 3 — Close the loop.** Einstein classifies and closes the case, then drafts reusable Knowledge.
- **Branded to the prospect** — the rep enters the account name and brand color and the whole workspace re-skins.
- **Zero infrastructure, zero risk** — one hosted page; fictional data, synthetic likenesses, no PII, not connected to a live org. Safe to hand to a customer.
- **Products surfaced and linked** — each tile lists the products powering it (Agentforce for Service, Agentforce Voice, Agentforce Contact Center, Service Cloud, Financial Services Cloud) with links to the product pages.
- **Usage analytics built in** — every run is logged (rep, account/opportunity, scenario, how far they got).

## 4 · The value narrative — seven pillars

*Lead each pillar with the outcome, then the mechanism, then the metric to watch. These are your talking points, ranked for an exec audience.*

### Pillar 1 — SE leverage (protect our scarcest resource)
- **Outcome:** SEs stop being the bottleneck for standard demos and redeploy hours to POCs, architecture, and must-win deals.
- **Mechanism:** Reps self-serve the demo; SEs are pulled in only when a deal earns it.
- **Metric to watch:** demos run without SE involvement; SE hours redeployed to late-stage technical wins.

### Pillar 2 — Pipeline velocity (demo in the moment of interest)
- **Outcome:** The demo happens in the *first* conversation, not days later — compressing early-stage cycle time.
- **Mechanism:** No org spin-up, no scheduling. Open a URL, brand it, go.
- **Metric to watch:** time-to-first-demo; meeting→opportunity conversion on demoed accounts.

### Pillar 3 — Consistency & message control (de-risk the demo)
- **Outcome:** Every rep tells the same grounded, on-message story — the quality floor rises.
- **Mechanism:** A guided, narrated flow with built-in talk tracks; the "aha" beats are engineered in, not improvised.
- **Metric to watch:** win rate on demoed deals; qualitative demo feedback.

### Pillar 4 — Enablement & faster ramp (a learning tool, not just a selling tool)
- **Outcome:** Reps — especially new hires — get fluent in the Agentforce Service platform fast, by *doing* the demo.
- **Mechanism:** Self-paced click-through with talk tracks and recorded call audio; the same asset trains and sells.
- **Metric to watch:** new-hire time-to-first-demo; % of field certified/comfortable on the FS Agentforce story.

### Pillar 5 — Visual, tangible discovery
- **Outcome:** Discovery stops being abstract. Reps anchor the conversation in the customer's own world — branded, industry-specific, and interactive.
- **Mechanism:** Industry + channel **filters** let a rep jump straight to the customer's scenario; branding personalizes it to the prospect.
- **Metric to watch:** scenario coverage by industry; engagement/attention in discovery calls.

### Pillar 6 — Product attach & education (connect the story to what we sell)
- **Outcome:** Every scenario makes the **buyable products** explicit and links reps (and customers) to the product pages.
- **Mechanism:** Expandable "product information" on each tile with direct links to Agentforce, Service Cloud, and Financial Services Cloud.
- **Metric to watch:** multi-product attach in demoed deals; click-through to product content.

### Pillar 7 — Adoption analytics & Voice of the Customer
- **Outcome:** Leadership gets a live read on adoption and demand signal — *who* is demoing, *for which accounts/opportunities*, *which industries and scenarios* land, and *how far* reps get.
- **Mechanism:** Fire-and-forget usage tracking to a central sheet; every run is attributed to a rep and (optionally) an opportunity.
- **Metric to watch:** weekly active reps; scenarios by industry; accounts/opps touched — a coaching and roadmap signal, not just a vanity count.

> **Bonus pillars to mention if asked:** near-zero cost to run and maintain; extensible by design (new industries, scenarios, and channels add quickly); and it's **safe to share externally** because there's no customer data and no live org.

## 5 · Future-proof by design — the roadmap becomes content

Because the demo is **data-driven and built from reusable "acts,"** new product releases slot in as new scenarios, new acts, or new channels — the tool grows with the Salesforce roadmap **without a rebuild.**

- **Agentic Service Portals** — add a self-service act where an Agentforce agent resolves the customer in a branded portal: deflection and containment first, then escalation with full context when a human is needed.
- **Proactive Service** — the co-worker already hints at it. A proactive scenario shows the agent reaching out *first* — a fraud alert, an outage, a renewal — turning service into a retention and growth motion.
- **Workforce Engagement Management (WEM)** — the demo already scores the call and notifies a supervisor. A WEM act adds the manager's world: forecasting, scheduling, and coaching driven by those interaction signals.
- **Across the platform** — Unified Knowledge, Einstein Conversation Mining, Data Cloud segmentation, and new channels (Chat, Email, SMS, WhatsApp are already stubbed in the UI) each become a tile or an act as they GA.

> **Why it lands with leadership:** this isn't a one-off demo we rebuild every launch. It's a **durable enablement surface** where every new release becomes content the field can run the week it ships.

## 9 · Anticipated questions & responses

**"Is this just a toy — does it actually help us win?"**
It's a *selling and enablement* asset. It gets a real, on-message demo into the first call (velocity), keeps the story consistent (win quality), and ramps reps (capacity). It doesn't replace a tailored SE demo on a complex deal — it *frees SEs* for exactly those.

**"Does this replace SEs?"**
No — it protects them. It handles the standard, repeatable demo so SEs spend their hours on discovery, POCs, and technical wins. Think of it as raising the floor, not lowering the ceiling.

**"What about data security and compliance?"**
There is no customer data and no live org. Everything is fictional data and synthetic likenesses, clearly labeled "for demonstration only." It's safe to run in front of — or hand to — a customer.

**"Who maintains it, and what does it cost to run?"**
It's a single hosted page with no backend and no license cost. The content is data-driven, so adding a scenario or industry is a contained change — not a rebuild.

**"How do we measure ROI?"**
Adoption analytics are built in: weekly active reps, scenarios by industry, and accounts/opportunities touched. Pair that with pipeline data (time-to-first-demo, conversion on demoed deals) and we can attribute impact, not assume it.

**"Can it scale beyond Financial Services?"**
Yes. The three-act flow and the data-driven structure are industry-agnostic; FS is the proof point. The same engine extends to other clouds and verticals.

**"Why not just use a standard demo org?"**
Orgs need provisioning, cleanup, and an SE to drive them, and they break. This runs anywhere instantly, brands to the customer, narrates itself, and can't be left in a bad state.
