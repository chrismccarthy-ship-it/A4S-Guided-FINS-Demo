# Talk Tracks — Agentforce Service for Financial Services

Five guided demo scenarios. Each is designed to run in **under 10 minutes** at a natural pace, following the same 24-step click-through in the app. Speak the **bold delivery lines**; the *(italics)* are stage cues telling you what to click. The whole demo is one continuous story: **an Agentforce Voice call → warm hand-off to a rep → a grounded Service Plan → wrap-up → case closed → new Knowledge drafted — all on first contact.**

**The three-act spine (same for all five):**
- **Act 1 — The monitored Voice call (Steps 1–7, ~2.5 min):** A customer is already talking to Agentforce Voice. The supervisor watches, then warm-transfers to a live rep.
- **Act 2 — The rep with full context (Steps 8–18, ~4.5 min):** The rep picks up with the full 360, drafts a Knowledge-grounded Service Plan, sends the article + email, and gets an automated wrap-up + scorecard.
- **Act 3 — Close the loop (Steps 19–24, ~2.5 min):** Einstein classifies and closes the case, then drafts a reusable Knowledge article.

**Pacing tip:** don't narrate every click. Land the *aha* moments — the warm hand-off (Step 8–9), the grounded citation (Step 13–14), the automated wrap-up + scorecard (Step 17–18), and the closed loop (Step 22–24). Let the screen do the work in between.
